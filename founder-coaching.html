Coaching or consulting?

Coaching means you do the work, with my help: I help you think clearly, decide well, and build the plan. Consulting means I run the work and own the outcome. Founders who want a thinking partner choose coaching; founders who want a programme delivered choose consulting. If you're not sure, say so on the call and I'll tell you which I'd pick.

Questions people ask about founder coaching

Do you coach founders and entrepreneurs?

Yes. Founders come to me for personal guidance, for help growing the business, or both. Founder coaching focuses on the person behind the business — mindset and confidence, decision-making and leadership, managing pressure and uncertainty, and building healthier routines so you do not burn out. Business coaching focuses on growing and scaling it. Most founders need a blend of both.

What is the difference between founder coaching and business coaching?

Founder coaching works on you: how you think, decide, lead and cope. Business coaching works on the company: growth strategy and go-to-market, customer experience and brand positioning, hiring and structuring teams, scaling operations, and using tech and AI where it pays back. As a business scales, leadership and personal balance start to matter as much as the commercial side, which is why most founders need some of each.
