About

Kajol Phadnis — strategy & growth consultant, physicist to operator

I'm a strategy and growth consultant and former Deloitte management consultant. I've spent over a decade turning one question — what does the customer actually want? — into launches, loyalty programmes and growth strategies that moved real numbers, across the UK, the Middle East and beyond.

Imperial College LondonPhysics

From physics to management consulting

I trained in physics at Imperial College London. Physics teaches you that every complex system has an underlying logic if you look hard enough — and it turns out customers work the same way. That instinct, to find the pattern and prove it with data, has shaped my whole career.

Eight yearsDeloitte Digital

Advising

I spent eight years at Deloitte Digital, where I led the Customer Experience & Product Strategy team, served as COO for Customer Strategy and Chief of Staff to the Head of Retail — ranked in the top 5% of my cohort five years running. I launched products into new markets, designed loyalty propositions, and coached CxOs through their first 180 days.

70+ brandsMiddle East retail

From advising to owning

Then I crossed to the other side of the table, leading customer experience and transformation for one of the Middle East's largest retail groups — 70+ US and UK brands, run across 13 Middle East markets, including names like Starbucks, Victoria's Secret, Shake Shack, American Eagle and Alo Yoga. When it's your P&L, your teams and your customers, you learn what advice actually survives contact with reality.

NowGrowthcraft

What I do now

Today I work in two ways. Through my consultancy, Growthcraft, I help businesses find and fix the thing holding back their growth — strategy, customer experience, customer growth and product, delivered end to end. And through my YouTube channel and newsletter I help a community of 17,000+ people build careers in consulting and corporate, with a particular focus on women navigating corporate and consulting.
