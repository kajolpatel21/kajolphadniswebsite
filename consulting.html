Kajol is a management consultant based between London and Dubai, and a former Deloitte management consultant. What does a growth consultant do?

A growth consultant helps a business work out where its next growth will come from — new customers, higher spend, better retention, new products or markets — quantifies it, and builds the plan to capture it. Kajol also leads delivery, running the programme and change work so the numbers actually move.

How is customer experience consulting different from growth strategy?

Growth strategy sets the direction and the commercial case; customer experience consulting designs the journeys, propositions and service that make it real for customers. Kajol works across both, connecting strategy to the moments that actually change customer behaviour and revenue.
